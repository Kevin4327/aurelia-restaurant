The Kitchen

Chef Elena Moretti & The Open Hearth

Opened in 2019 just blocks from the harbor, Aurelia centers around a custom 650°F white-oak hearth built by Santa Barbara stonemasons. Every morning begins with channel-boat deliveries of halibut, rockfish, and sea urchin paired with biodynamic produce from Carpinteria and Santa Ynez farms.

Whether dining at our 8-seat Chef's Counter, hosting an intimate gathering in the Olive Courtyard, or ordering online with live delivery tracking, our promise stays unchanged: honest coastal ingredients treated with restraint and warmth.
